## 5. Assessment & Verdict

### Is the strategy broadly distributed or winner-dependent?

The Gini coefficient of 0.541 indicates MODERATE return concentration. The strategy has meaningful dependence on its top winners, but returns are not entirely dominated by a handful of stocks.

Only **36 stocks** (of 332 ever held) are needed to generate 50% of total return.
**83 stocks** are needed to generate 80% of total return.

### Robustness after winner exclusion

After removing the top 10 contributors, the strategy still delivers 39.8% CAGR with Sharpe 1.310. **The strategy is ROBUST to winner exclusion.**
Even after removing the top 20 contributors, CAGR is 36.0% (Sharpe 1.200). The factor continues to generate alpha from the broader universe.

### Survivorship bias implication

The top contributors in this backtest are stocks that survived AND thrived over 2010-2026. In live trading:
- Some future top-20 momentum stocks will delist, merge, or enter bear markets.
- The realized concentration is likely HIGHER than what is observable ex-ante.
- This makes the strategy more fragile than the backtest concentration implies.

**Practical recommendation:** Monitor rolling 6-month Sharpe per stock. If a single stock accounts for >15% of month's portfolio return for 3+ months, consider a concentration cap (e.g., max 10% per stock) to reduce dependence on any single winner.
